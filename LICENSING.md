## Exclusions

The repository must not contain raw payloads or redistributable copies from a
market-data provider unless that provider's terms explicitly permit it. Names,
logos, trademarks, and service marks remain the property of their respective
owners. A file carrying its own license or attribution notice keeps that
notice and takes precedence over this repository-level policy.

Contributors must document third-party inputs and redistribution rights in the
benchmark's data card before publication.
